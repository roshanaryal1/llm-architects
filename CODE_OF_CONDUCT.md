# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This is a small research repository. The standard below applies to issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other project space.

## Standard

Be respectful and constructive. We are here to compare machine-generated architectures, not to
attack the people who ran the prompts or the vendors whose systems produced the responses.

Specifically:

- **Critique output, not contributors.** "This recommendation fabricates a tool" is fine.
  "The submitter is careless" is not.
- **Vendor-neutral.** Findings — including unflattering ones — are reported the same way for every
  system. No brand advocacy, no brand bashing. Let the rubric and the checks speak.
- **Accurate attribution.** Do not misrepresent which system produced which output, or alter a
  captured response to make a point.
- **No harassment**, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Raise concerns via a private message to the maintainer (GitHub profile contact) or a confidential
issue. The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this standard, and may
block repeat offenders.
