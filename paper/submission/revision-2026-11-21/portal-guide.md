## Figures (issue 14)

Upload the PNG files from `paper/figures/` (rendered at 3x; the SVG files are the vector
originals). Check the Cureus figure-format requirements on the author dashboard before upload.

**Figure 1** (`fig1-response-totals.png`). Rubric total per response. Dot: adjudicated total
out of 18. Bar: lowest to highest total from the four raters before adjudication. Dashed lines
separate the rank-based thirds. Non-anchor captures only.

**Figure 2** (`fig2-memory-breakeven.png`). Break-even non-model reserve per design: 32 GB
minus the stated co-resident weights minus the key-value cache. Dot: the estimator's default
key-value rate; bar: rate doubled to halved. A design fits on a host whose non-model reserve is
left of its dot. Dashed lines: 7 GB lean headless host; 11.5 GB estimator default with a
browser. Asterisk: an alternative reading of a response, not its stated design. DeepSeek
Instant's stated design swaps one large model at a time and appears as two phases (qwen,
gemma). Sizes are as each response states them; the Zhipu (zai) sizes are known to be wrong.

**Figure 3** (`fig3-consensus-split.png`). Modal agreement per decision axis across the 10
non-anchor systems, structural axes (24) and product axes (9). Each dot is one axis; the black
bar marks the group median. Agreement is not evidence of correctness.

**Figure 4** (`fig4-dimension-agreement.png`). Per-dimension Cohen's kappa (unweighted)
between the author-derived baseline and GPT-5.6 Sol over 13 responses. Bar: 95% percentile
bootstrap interval from 10,000 resamples of responses.
